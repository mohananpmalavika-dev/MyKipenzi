# Privacy Settings - User Guide

## Accessing Privacy Settings

1. **Open Settings**
   - Click the ⚙️ Settings icon in the bottom navigation rail
   - OR click on your profile avatar

2. **Find Privacy Settings**
   - Scroll down past your profile photo and voice settings
   - Look for the "Privacy Settings" section
   - Two dropdown menus control your visibility

## Privacy Controls

### Who can see when I'm online

This controls who sees the green "● online" indicator next to your name.

**Options:**
- **Everyone** - All users can see when you're actively using the app
- **My contacts** - Only people you've chatted with can see your online status
- **Nobody** - Your online status is hidden from everyone

**When are you "online"?**
You're marked as online when you've used the app within the last 60 seconds. This includes:
- Viewing your conversations
- Reading messages
- Sending messages
- Making calls

### Who can see my last seen time

This controls who sees when you were last active (e.g., "last seen 5m ago").

**Options:**
- **Everyone** - All users can see when you last used the app
- **My contacts** - Only people you've chatted with can see your last active time
- **Nobody** - Your last seen time is hidden from everyone

**How is last seen calculated?**
Your last seen time updates automatically when you:
- Open your conversations list
- View messages in a chat
- The time shows relative to now: "5m ago", "2h ago", "yesterday", etc.

## Where You'll See Status

### User Directory
When browsing users to start a conversation:
- Online users show: **● online** in green
- Offline users show: **last seen X ago** in gray
- Hidden status shows: just name and handle

### Conversations List
In your chat list:
- Small green dot (●) next to online contacts
- "last seen X ago" below their language preference
- No indicator if privacy restricts viewing

### Chat Header
When chatting with someone:
- Shows "online" at the top if they're active
- Shows "last seen X ago" if they're not currently online
- No status if their privacy settings hide it

## Understanding Contact Privacy

**What is a "contact"?**
Someone is your contact if you have an existing conversation with them. This means:
- You've started a chat with them, OR
- They've started a chat with you

**Why does this matter?**
When you set privacy to "My contacts":
- Only people you've chatted with can see your status
- Random users browsing the directory won't see your status
- It's a good balance between privacy and staying connected

## Privacy Tips

### Maximum Privacy
Set both to **"Nobody"**
- Use the app completely privately
- No one sees when you're online or last active
- Best for: Users who want complete control over their availability

### Balanced Privacy  
Set both to **"My contacts"**
- Share status only with people you know
- Strangers can't track your activity
- Best for: Most users who want reasonable privacy

### Maximum Visibility
Set both to **"Everyone"**
- Anyone can see when you're online or last active
- Good for meeting new people
- Best for: Users who want to be easily discoverable

### Mixed Settings
You can mix and match:
- Example: Online status = "Nobody", Last seen = "My contacts"
- This hides your live online status but shows when you were last active to contacts
- Customize based on your comfort level

## Important Notes

**Your Privacy, Your Choice**
- Settings can be changed anytime
- Changes take effect immediately
- Your contacts won't be notified when you change settings

**Default Settings**
- New accounts default to "Everyone" for both settings
- This gives maximum visibility initially
- Change them as soon as you want more privacy

**What Others See**
- If your settings block someone from seeing your status, they see nothing
- No placeholder like "hidden" or "private" - just no status indicator
- They can still message you normally

**Activity Updates**
- Your last seen updates automatically - you don't need to do anything
- The system tracks when you view conversations or messages
- Sending messages also updates your last seen time

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I see who's viewing my profile?**
A: No, this feature only controls who sees your status, not who views you.

**Q: Will my contacts know I changed my privacy settings?**
A: No, settings changes are private and not notified to anyone.

**Q: If I set to "Nobody", can people still message me?**
A: Yes! Privacy settings only affect status visibility, not messaging.

**Q: What's the difference between online and last seen?**
A: "Online" means you're active right now. "Last seen" shows when you were last active if you're not currently online.

**Q: Does reading messages in notifications update my last seen?**
A: Only when you open the app and view conversations or messages. Notification previews don't update your status.

**Q: Can I temporarily hide my status?**
A: Yes, just change to "Nobody" temporarily, then change back when you want.

**Q: Will this affect video/voice calls?**
A: No, call functionality works the same regardless of privacy settings.
