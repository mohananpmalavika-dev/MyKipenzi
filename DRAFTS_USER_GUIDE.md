# Message Drafts - User Guide 📝

## What are Message Drafts?

Message Drafts automatically save your unfinished messages so you never lose your thoughts when switching between conversations or closing the app.

## How It Works

### ✍️ Writing a Draft

1. **Start typing** your message in any conversation
2. **Pause for a second** - your draft is automatically saved
3. **No action needed** - everything happens in the background!

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 💬 Chat with Sarah                  │
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                     │
│ [Message input]                     │
│ "Hey, I was thinking about..."     │ ← You type here
│                                     │
│ ⏱️  *1 second passes*               │
│ ✅ Draft saved automatically!       │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 🔄 Draft is Saved

Once saved, your draft is:
- **Protected** - Won't be lost if you close the app
- **Per-conversation** - Each chat has its own draft
- **Private** - Only you can see your drafts

### 👀 Seeing Your Drafts

Conversations with drafts show a special indicator:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 💬 Conversations                    │
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                     │
│ 👤 Sarah Johnson                    │
│ 📝 Draft: Hey, I was thinking...   │ ← Red italic text
│ Malayalam · 2 hours ago             │
│                                     │
│ 👤 John Smith                       │
│ Great! See you tomorrow 🌟          │ ← Normal message
│ English · Yesterday                 │
│                                     │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 📱 Returning to Your Draft

1. **Open** the conversation with a saved draft
2. **Your text appears** automatically in the message box
3. **Continue typing** right where you left off!

```
You left:        "Hey, I was thinking about..."
                          ↓
You return:      "Hey, I was thinking about our plans"
                 Continue typing here! →
```

### 📤 Sending Your Message

When you send your message:
- ✅ Message is sent to your contact
- ✅ Draft is automatically cleared
- ✅ Conversation shows the sent message

```
Before send:  📝 Draft: Hey, I was thinking...
After send:   ✉️  Hey, I was thinking about our plans
```

## 🎯 Tips & Tricks

### When Drafts Are Useful
- **Long messages** - Take your time, no rush!
- **Interruptions** - Phone call? No problem, your draft is saved
- **Multiple chats** - Switch between conversations freely
- **Deep thoughts** - Come back later to finish your message

### Draft Best Practices
- **Don't worry about saving** - It happens automatically
- **Check before sending** - Review your draft when you return
- **Clear old drafts** - Just send or delete the text

## ⏰ Draft Expiration

Drafts are automatically cleaned up after **30 days** to keep your app running smoothly.

```
Day 1:  ✅ Draft saved
Day 15: ✅ Draft still available
Day 30: ✅ Draft still available  
Day 31: 🗑️  Draft automatically removed
```

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

Your drafts are:
- ✅ **Private** - Only visible to you
- ✅ **Secure** - Stored safely on the server
- ✅ **Deleted** - Removed when conversation is deleted
- ✅ **Never shared** - Recipients can't see your drafts

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: How do I save a draft?
**A:** Just start typing! Drafts save automatically after 1 second.

### Q: Can I have multiple drafts?
**A:** Yes! Each conversation can have its own draft.

### Q: What happens if I send the message?
**A:** The draft is automatically cleared.

### Q: Can the other person see my draft?
**A:** No, drafts are completely private.

### Q: What if I don't want a draft anymore?
**A:** Just delete all the text from the message box, or send an empty message won't work.

### Q: Do drafts work offline?
**A:** Currently, drafts are saved to the server, so you need an internet connection.

### Q: How long are drafts kept?
**A:** Drafts are kept for 30 days, then automatically deleted.

### Q: Can I see all my drafts at once?
**A:** Look for the 📝 indicator in your conversation list!
